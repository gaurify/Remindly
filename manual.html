Manual & FAQs

Everything you need to set up hydration reminders and get the most out of Remindly.

Getting started

1. Create an account

Go to Get started, enter an email and password, and sign up. Confirm your email if prompted, then log in.

2. Set your hydration goal

On your dashboard, open Reminder settings and set a daily goal in millilitres. 2,000 ml is a common starting point, but any goal works.

3. Choose a reminder interval

Pick every 30 minutes, hourly, every 2 hours, or set a custom interval. You can also set a start and end time so reminders only happen during your day.

4. Enable notifications

Click Enable notifications on your dashboard and allow the browser prompt. This is what lets Remindly reach you after you close the tab.

Using the dashboard

Recording water

Use the +100 ml, +250 ml, or +500 ml buttons to log water as you drink it. Your progress ring and totals update immediately.

Understanding your progress

The dashboard shows today's total, your remaining amount, and your progress as a percentage. Totals reset automatically at the start of each new day.

Next reminder & reminder status

Your dashboard always shows when your next reminder is due, and whether notifications are currently enabled.

How reminders work

While Remindly is open

You'll see an animated pop-out reminder appear directly on the page when it's your time to drink.

After you close the tab

Once notifications are enabled, Remindly can send a real browser push notification even when the site isn't open — this requires the one-time notification permission described above.

FAQ

What is Remindly?

A web-based hydration reminder. It tracks your water intake against a daily goal and reminds you to drink at intervals you choose.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Remindly runs entirely in your browser — there's no download or app store required.

What happens if I don't allow notifications?

Remindly still works while the tab is open — you'll see the on-page pop-out reminder. Without notification permission, you won't get reminded once you close the tab.

I blocked notifications by accident — how do I fix it?

Open your browser's site settings for Remindly (usually via the padlock icon in the address bar), find Notifications, and set it back to "Allow." Then refresh the dashboard.

Is my data private?

Your hydration logs and settings are tied to your account and protected by row-level security, so only you can read or write your own data.

Where can I find project updates?

Check the GitHub repository for the latest changes.
